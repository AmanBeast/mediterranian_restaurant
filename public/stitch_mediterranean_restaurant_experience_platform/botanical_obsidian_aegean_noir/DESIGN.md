## Brand & Style
The design system articulates an intimate, sensory hospitality experience blending Mediterranean hearth traditions with late-night craft mixology. The audience comprises discerning epicures, cocktail enthusiasts, and evening patrons seeking refined leisure, tactile indulgence, and an evocative, nocturnal ambiance.

The aesthetic fuses **sensory dark-mode luxury** with **translucent glassmorphism** and high-end editorial curation. Depth is generated not by stark structural boundaries, but by layered twilight surfaces, botanical depths, and warm mineral metallics. The visual presence balances quiet restraint with lush, artisanal details: smoke-tinted glass overlays, warm ivory type that avoids sterile high contrast, and sun-drenched metallic accents reminiscent of olive groves, hammered copper cookware, and antique gold cocktail tools.

## Colors
The palette evokes dusk on the Mediterranean coastline—shadowed olive groves, charred hearth embers, and candlelight reflecting off aged brass. 

- **Primary (`#3E5434`)**: Saturated Mediterranean olive leaf. Anchors brand badges, interactive focus indicators, and botanical identifiers.
- **Secondary (`#D47D57`)**: Warm terracotta copper. Used for high-priority calls to action (e.g., "Reserve a Table"), culinary distinctions, and warm active triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#E5A93C`)**: Sunlit heirloom gold. Applied sparingly to highlight cocktail ratings, artisanal vintage years, tasting notes, and delicate borders.
- **Neutral & Surfaces (`#0C100D` to `#18221B`)**: Obsidian charcoal steeped with sub-surface Aegean green tones. Creates rich dark depth without sterile pitch-black flattening.
- **Text & Accents (`#F3EEE7`, `#DED7CB`)**: Champagne linen and warm limestone. Delivers high legibility and relaxed optical warmth across atmospheric dark layers.

## Typography
Typography creates an editorial dialogue between the romantic warmth of classical Mediterranean culinary journals and the surgical precision of modern mixology.

- **Headlines & Display (`Playfair Display`)**: Used for hero introductions, course headers, cocktail designations, and narrative quotes. Generous optical sizes capture elegance through high-contrast strokes and sculpted serifs.
- **Body & Structural Elements (`Manrope`)**: Provides effortless scannability for ingredients, tasting profiles, preparation methods, and digital booking interfaces. Its geometric balance ensures legibility against darkened backdrops.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Employs uppercase letter-spacing (0.08em to 0.12em) in small-scale Manrope to evoke curated spirit bottles, vintage cellar ledgers, and bespoke menus.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column grid on desktop displays with asymmetric content groupings to mirror upscale editorial magazine layouts. Responsive scaling compresses the structure gracefully into an 8-column layout on tablet viewports and a singular 4-column flow on mobile screens.

- **Horizontal Bleed & Margins**: Outer canvas margins expand generously to `3rem` on desktop, giving dishes and libations expansive visual room.
- **Vertical Flow Rhythm**: Section breaks leverage dynamic rhythm spacing (`space-xl` combined with multi-step multiples up to 6rem) to differentiate culinary courses (Raw Bar, Hearth, Cocktails, Digestifs) without relying on heavy structural dividers.
- **Reflow Rules**: Multi-column tasting menus and split drink profiles stack vertically on screens narrower than 768px, ensuring item names, botanical notes, and prices retain uninterrupted alignment.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids cold drop shadows, prioritizing translucent botanical glassmorphism, surface tonal tiers, and ambient candlelight glows.

1. **Surface Base (`#0C100D`)**: The primary canvas, evoking dark volcanic stone and evening light.
2. **Elevated Containers (`#121814`)**: Slightly raised surfaces for menu cards and interactive booking stages.
3. **Glassmorphic Paneling**: Frosted overlays utilize `rgba(18, 24, 20, 0.65)` combined with a 16px to 24px backdrop blur and a fine hairline rim (`1px solid rgba(222, 215, 203, 0.12)`).
4. **Ambient Lighting**: Floating interactive modals and spotlighted signature cocktails cast an ultra-soft, warm radiant glow using diffused amber or gold tints (`box-shadow: 0 16px 48px -8px rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.08)`).

## Shapes
A disciplined, architectural curvature level is applied throughout the system (`roundedness: 1`). Radii of 4px (`0.25rem`) define standard inputs, tags, and cards, scaling to 8px (`0.5rem`) on hero feature modules and sheet modals. This tailored corner style balances Mediterranean stone geometry with contemporary lounge luxury, strictly avoiding overly playful bubble aesthetics in favor of crisp, timeless poise.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary ("Reserve", "Order Selection")*: Styled with terracotta copper (`#D47D57`) fill, obsidian charcoal text, and subtle hover transition to sunlit gold (`#E5A93C`).
  - *Secondary ("Explore Cocktails", "Private Events")*: Frosted dark glass base, 1px border in `rgba(222, 215, 203, 0.25)`, champagne text, brightening slightly upon hover.
  - *Text Actions*: Champagne text with a continuous subtle golden baseline indicator.

- **Chips & Dietary Tags**:
  - Encased in deep olive tones (`rgba(64, 93, 69, 0.3)`) framed with fine 1px strokes. Type rendered in Manrope label-md uppercase (`#DED7CB`).

- **Menu Lists & Tasting Rows**:
  - Clean horizontal arrangements punctuated with subtle dotted or shaded champagne dividers. Dish titles feature Playfair Display serif paired with price callouts in terracotta copper. Botanical and allergen notes sit below in Manrope body-sm.

- **Form Fields & Reservation Inputs**:
  - Recessed obsidian surface (`#0C100D`) with a 1px border of `rgba(222, 215, 203, 0.15)`. Focus states activate a sunlit gold halo (`#E5A93C`) and crisp border brightening without default browser outline artifacts.

- **Checkboxes & Radios**:
  - Custom square-soft (4px) and circular enclosures finished in dark Aegean green, checked states illuminate in warm terracotta with ivory check glyphs.

- **Cards (Cocktail Profiles & Hearth Specialties)**:
  - Frosted charcoal-green bodies (`rgba(18, 24, 20, 0.7)`), 1px subtle champagne-tinted borders, displaying vivid food/drink imagery framed by ample interior padding (`space-lg`).

- **Domain-Specific Components**:
  - *Cocktail Note Badges*: Small circular or lozenge badges displaying spirit profiles (e.g., "Mastic & Thyme", "Smoked Fig", "Orange Blossom") with soft amber perimeter glows.
  - *Table Availability Selector*: Atmospheric time-slot pill grid using olive-tinted active outlines and champagne selection indicators.
