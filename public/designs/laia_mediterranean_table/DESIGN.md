## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a refined Mediterranean coastal spirit—rooted in natural hospitality, culinary provenance, and understated European elegance. It speaks to discerning patrons seeking slow-food experiences, bespoke dining events, and curated reservations. The emotional response is welcoming, sun-drenched, tactile, and grounded, evoking an afternoon on an Aegean terrace shaded by olive branches.

The visual direction merges **Editorial Hospitality** with **Tactile Warmth**. It rejects sterile, tech-heavy interfaces in favor of generous margins, linen-like whitespace, bespoke botanical motifs, and rich, organic color blocks. Interfaces feel physically tangible, like high-end printed tasting menus and architectural ceramics, while upholding modern accessibility standards and intuitive usability.

## Colors

The palette takes direct inspiration from Aegean natural materials, olive groves, and sundrenched coastal towns. 

- **Primary (`#3A4D39` - Deep Olive):** Anchors navigation, dominant action states, and key display elements. Carries the natural weight of ancient olive branches.
- **Secondary (`#C86446` - Warm Terracotta):** Used for focal CTAs (such as "Reserve a Table"), active indicators, and high-energy highlights reminiscent of kiln-fired earth.
- **Tertiary (`#1E3A4C` - Aegean Sea Blue):** Brings depth and contrast, serving as an evening-mode alternate, rich banner background, or footer base.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` - Cream):** Forms the foundational surface background, softening screens into the texture of raw limestone and hand-pressed linen.
- **Supporting Tones:** Sand/Warm Beige (`#F0EBE1`) handles surface-level container fills and muted dividers; Muted Sage (`#8F9E8B`) delivers subdued badge indicators and secondary tags; Sun-Drenched Amber (`#E5A93C`) acts as a selective accent for culinary accolades, star ratings, and chef selections.

## Typography

The type scale juxtaposes the high-contrast literary qualities of **Playfair Display** with the clear, rhythmic ergonomics of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Headlines & Display:** Playfair Display introduces editorial gravitas for menus, section headers, and hero story-telling. It evokes bespoke gastronomy journals.
- **Body & Captions:** Plus Jakarta Sans ensures immediate readability for dish descriptions, allergy warnings, pricing matrices, and reservation flows.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered with elevated letter spacing and medium-to-bold weights to sustain crisp hierarchy on physical tickets and digital viewports alike.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a responsive 12-column grid on desktop screens, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet devices and a 4-column layout on mobile viewports. Generous vertical breathing room replicates the unhurried cadence of Mediterranean dining.

Margins stay expansive on larger displays (`margin`: 3rem) to preserve an editorial border around content containers, collapsing smoothly on handheld devices (`margin-mobile`: 1.25rem). Gutters provide clean separation between wine lists, gallery assets, and booking inputs without breaking structural cohesion.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **Tonal Layers** combined with **Warm Ambient Shadows**. Sharp, cool drop shadows are avoided; instead, depth mimics natural sunlight casting soft, diffused shadows over warm clay surfaces.

- **Base Canvas:** Neutral cream (`#FAF7F2`) acts as ground zero.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Course Listings):** Tinted warm sand (`#F0EBE1`) or pure white overlaid on cream with a 1px border tinted in Deep Olive at 8% opacity (`rgba(58, 77, 57, 0.08)`).
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Dropdowns, Floating Navigation, Reservation Modals):** Suspended with a multi-layered soft drop shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(30, 58, 76, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(200, 100, 70, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Overlays, Drawer Menus):** Backdrop blurs (4px) with warm-toned scrims (`rgba(30, 58, 76, 0.3)`).

## Shapes

The design system incorporates soft geometric curvature (`roundedness: 2`, base radius `0.5rem` / 8px) balancing architectural precision with the hand-molded quality of Mediterranean ceramics. 

Small interactive elements (badges, micro-buttons) adopt standard 8px radii. Larger containers, cards, and modal sheets scale to `1rem` (16px) or `1.5rem` (24px) to retain an inviting, tactile softness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Reservations, Order Finalization):** Background in Warm Terracotta (`#C86446`), text in Neutral Cream (`#FAF7F2`), rounded to 8px. Hover state shifts to a deeper burnished tone (`#B25337`) with a subtle Y-axis translation (-1px).
- **Secondary Action (Wine Pairing, Private Dining Inquiries):** Deep Olive (`#3A4D39`) solid fill or high-contrast 1.5px border over Neutral Cream with Deep Olive label.
- **Tertiary (Text Links):** Playfair Display italic or bold Plus Jakarta Sans with a terracotta underline offset by 4px.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- Encapsulated pills (`rounded-xl` or fully rounded) filled with Sand/Warm Beige (`#F0EBE1`), framed by a delicate border (`#8F9E8B` at 30% opacity). Text rendered in Deep Olive with `label-sm` typography. Selected filters turn solid Deep Olive with cream typography.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background set to pure white or Sand/Warm Beige, 8px radius, bordered with 1px `rgba(58, 77, 57, 0.2)`. 
- Focus state brings a 1.5px Warm Terracotta outline and a gentle terracotta ambient glow, eschewing standard browser-blue rings. Labels sit anchored above the field in `label-md`.

### Cards & Menu Listings
- **Tasting Menu Cards:** Sand/Warm Beige fill, 16px corner radius, framed by a muted limestone hairline rule. Course names leverage `headline-sm` in Deep Olive, while ingredients use `body-sm` in a softened slate-olive tint. Pricing is right-aligned in `title-md`.
- **Visual Highlight Cards:** Immersive imagery clipped with 16px corners, overlaying subtle linear gradients transitioning to Deep Olive or Aegean Sea Blue at the base for contrast behind white text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Custom 20px inputs with 4px border radius for checkboxes and circular for radios. Borders styled in `#3A4D39` (Deep Olive). Checked states fill with Warm Terracotta featuring a crisp, cream-colored checkmark or center pip.

### Dining-Specific Components
- **Time-Slot Selector:** Clean grid of pill buttons displaying reservation times. Filled in `#FAF7F2`, bordered in subtle sage; active selected time slot is filled in Warm Terracotta (`#C86446`) with high-contrast cream numerals.
- **Sommelier Notes Drawer:** A contextual slide-out tray leveraging Aegean Sea Blue (`#1E3A4C`) backgrounds, gold accent typography (`#E5A93C`), and cream body text for rare vintage descriptions.
